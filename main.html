About EPS

Judge the Future of Engineering
See innovation in action at the Engineering Project Showcase, where over 1,500 senior engineering students unveil solutions to real-world industry challenges. As a Showcase Judge, you'll get a front-row seat to fresh ideas, discover top emerging talent, and play a key role in recognizing the next generation of engineers.

Showcase Goals

The Engineering Project Showcase is designed to strengthen industry connections, inspire future engineers, and celebrate the innovation of our students.
Student Innovation: Recognize and celebrate the creativity, technical expertise, and problem-solving achievements of Texas A&M engineering students.
Industry Engagement: Demonstrate the impact of industry-sponsored capstone projects and strengthen connections between companies and engineering talent.
Retention: Inspire freshmen and sophomores by highlighting real-world projects that show the value of staying on the engineering pathway.
Recruitment: Spark interest in STEM among prospective students and educators by showcasing the breadth and impact of engineering solutions.

For additional information, please contact Industry and Nonprofit Partnerships (INP) at:

inp@tamu.edu

Student Team Participants

The Engineering Project Showcase is Texas A&M University's premier event celebrating the innovation and achievements of senior engineering students. More than 1,500 students from across multiple engineering disciplines present their capstone design projects, developed to address real-world challenges with creativity, technical expertise, and problem-solving skills. Participants represent a wide range of majors—including AERO, BAEN, BMEN, CHEN, CSCE, ECEN, ESET, ISEN, ITDE, MEEN, MMET, MSEN, MXET and more—demonstrating the breadth of engineering talent at Texas A&M. The Showcase not only highlights student accomplishment but also provides a platform for industry, faculty, and the community to engage with the next generation of engineering leaders.

Industry Judges

Industry representatives are invited to join us in person as Showcase Judges. Experience firsthand the innovative approaches student teams use to solve real-world challenges, provide valuable feedback that shapes their professional growth, and help us recognize this year's top projects. Serving as a judge also offers a unique opportunity to see how organizations leverage capstone projects to generate fresh insights, explore solutions, and connect with emerging engineering talent.
